# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want honestlint to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming otherwise.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when representing the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer, [@haritel97](https://github.com/haritel97). All reports will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential. Maintainers may remove comments, commits, or issues, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors who break this code.
